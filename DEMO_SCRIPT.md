# Gitex Africa 2026 Demo Script

## 2-Minute Live Sequence

### 0:00 to 0:20
Open `/`.

Talk track:

"HayatID gives a patient a QR or NFC emergency identity card so critical information becomes visible in seconds, even without an app."

Point to:

- hero statement
- profile CTAs
- scan simulation

### 0:20 to 0:40
Click the live scan demo and open `/u/demo-token`.

Talk track:

"This is the most important moment. In an emergency, blood type, allergies, medication context and a contact are visible immediately."

Point to:

- blood type
- allergy pills
- bystander instructions
- fixed call button

### 0:40 to 0:55
Mention professional access on the same page.

Talk track:

"Anyone gets level 0. A verified healthcare professional can unlock level 1 through a controlled flow. In this demo the OTP is mocked, but the architecture is already backend-first."

### 0:55 to 1:15
Open `/register` then `/dashboard`.

Talk track:

"The patient flow is simple: create the profile, receive the card, and then manage access history, completion, and privacy actions from the dashboard."

Point to:

- multi-step registration
- emergency preview
- profile completion bar
- QR card panel
- access history timeline

### 1:15 to 1:35
Open `/pro` then `/pro/dashboard`.

Talk track:

"For doctors and pharmacists, we keep the public layer simple and the professional layer traceable, with KYC, access history and active delegations."

Point to:

- level 0 vs level 1 explanation
- KYC status
- access history table
- delegations widget

### 1:35 to 1:50
Open `/b2b`.

Talk track:

"The go-to-market starts with patients, but scales through clinics and pharmacies that become founder partners in pilots."

Point to:

- founder partner cards
- partnership CTA
- roadmap narrative

### 1:50 to 2:00
Finish on `/admin` or return to `/demo`.

Talk track:

"This is not just a nice front end. It already tells a credible operational story around trust, verification and controlled access, while staying safe for demo use."
